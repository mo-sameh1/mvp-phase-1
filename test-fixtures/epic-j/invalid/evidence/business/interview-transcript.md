# Business Interview Transcript

Source type: SME interview transcript
System: demo-legacy-system

Interviewer: Who uses the legacy permit system?

SME: The Citizen Applicant submits the permit request and waits for updates. A Permit Officer reviews
the submitted documents and either requests changes or approves the case.

Interviewer: What is the main business process?

SME: We call it the Permit Review Process in business procedure documents. Older integration notes
write the same process name without spaces as PermitReviewProcess, but both names refer to the same
business process.

Interviewer: What business service is provided?

SME: The office provides a Permit Application Service to citizens. The service depends on the review
process and the case-handling application.
