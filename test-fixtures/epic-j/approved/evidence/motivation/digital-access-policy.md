# Digital Access Policy Notes

Source type: internal policy summary
System: demo-legacy-system

The municipal permitting team has a regulatory compliance driver requiring every submitted permit
case to keep an auditable review trail. The office also has a citizen access goal: applicants should
be able to submit a permit request online and later see the case status without calling the service
desk.

The modernization sponsor stated that the MVP must preserve evidence links from any generated model
element back to the source material used to identify it.
